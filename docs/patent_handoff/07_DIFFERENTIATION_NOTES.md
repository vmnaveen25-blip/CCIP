## Strategic Distinctions for Prior-Art Rebuttals

When drafting or defending claims against anticipated prior art rejections (e.g. under 35 U.S.C. 102/103 or EPC Art 54/56), emphasize the following structural differentiations:

### 1. Rebuttal to Generic SIEM / SOAR Art (Splunk, IBM QRadar, Microsoft Sentinel)
- **Prior Art Reality:** Ingests machine logs (syslog, NetFlow, firewall alerts) within an enterprise network.
- **Invention Distinction:** Ingests unstructured citizen human-written complaints and evidence; operates a dual-stage hybrid NER enforcing structured indicator authority; correlates citizen dockets across diverse reporting victims rather than monitoring server infrastructure.

### 2. Rebuttal to Generic Police RMS / Case Management Systems
- **Prior Art Reality:** Relational CRUD databases for logging police incident forms and manual officer notes; contains no active neural inference or automated intelligence orchestration.
- **Invention Distinction:** Integrates 11 local enclave models generating automated technical extractions, vector similarities, candidate correlations, and evidence graphs while preserving human officer review dispositions against automated model drift.

### 3. Rebuttal to Standalone Phishing Checkers (VirusTotal, URLScan, PhishTank)
- **Prior Art Reality:** Isolated URL/domain reputation lookups that return binary maliciousness scores to public users without downstream reporting capabilities.
- **Invention Distinction:** Seamlessly bridges pre-incident explainable screening to official law-enforcement complaint generation, pre-filling structured technical indicators into official case records.

### 4. Rebuttal to Standard Continual-Learning / MLOps Literature
- **Prior Art Reality:** Focuses on autonomous model retraining via online learning, active learning sampling, or unverified crowd-sourced feedback.
- **Invention Distinction:** Implements a deterministic 5-hard-gate filter as a non-bypassable barrier; stages approved records into a quarantined queue under exclusive AI Lead oversight; architecturally prohibits autonomous retraining in public-safety workflows.
