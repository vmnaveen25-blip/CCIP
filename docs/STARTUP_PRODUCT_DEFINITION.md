# Startup Product Definition: CCIP / Astraea Enclave

**Product Name:** Citizen Cybercrime Intelligence & Reporting Platform (CCIP)  
**Commercial Brand:** Astraea Enclave  
**Product Stage:** Advanced Prototype / Pilot Preparation  
**Document Purpose:** Commercial Positioning, Value Proposition, and Market Segmentation  

---

## 1. Core Value Proposition

> **"Astraea Enclave transforms fragmented citizen cybercrime reports into structured, evidence-linked syndicate intelligence while preserving strict human investigative authority within a 100% sovereign, offline environment."**

Existing cybercrime reporting systems are passive digital dropboxes: citizens file static complaints, and police dockets remain isolated. Astraea Enclave bridges this divide through real-time citizen threat screening, hybrid entity extraction, multi-modal cross-case correlation, and interactive evidence graphs—reducing investigation triage times by an estimated 60% while strictly preventing automated law-enforcement decisions.

## 3. Product Modules Overview

1. **Citizen Cyber Shield:** Public-facing threat assistant providing pre-incident explainable threat analysis on URLs, SMS smishing, and phishing emails, linked to one-click complaint filing.
2. **Intelligent Ingestion & Cryptographic Evidence Vault:** Structured intake with automatic SHA-256 cryptographic digest calculation, verifying evidence integrity across the investigation lifecycle.
3. **Hybrid Cyber NER & Entity Engine:** Dual-stage indicator extractor combining regex structured authority with DeBERTa-v3 token classification.
4. **Investigation Intelligence Orchestrator:** Multi-model pipeline generating multi-label crime classification, relation inference, urgency scoring, and semantic case similarity.
5. **Cross-Case Syndicate Correlator:** Tri-layered correlation engine combining vector proximity, physical indicator overlap, and transformer sequence classification.
6. **Interactive Evidence Graph Canvas:** Force-directed relational network visualizer rendering cases, suspects, payment handles, and predicted links with investigator verdict locking.
7. **Reliability-Gated Continual-Learning Pipeline:** Programmatic 5-hard-gate filter feeding a quarantined candidate queue under exclusive AI Lead curation.
8. **Decoupled Five-Role Governance Console:** Comprehensive administrative, security, and audit interface enforcing strict separation between case investigation and model oversight.

---

## 4. Responsible AI Posture

- **Zero Autonomous Coercive Power:** Astraea Enclave cannot issue freeze notices, execute arrests, or dispatch officers. All AI outputs are labeled as *Candidate Correlations* or *AI Suggested Entities*.
- **100% Data Sovereignty:** Operates strictly on local on-premise hardware or private government cloud (`local_files_only=True`). Zero citizen data is sent to external cloud AI APIs.
- **Permanent Human Primacy:** Automated rescans are architecturally barred from overwriting human investigator determinations.
