# Phase L Comprehensive Risk Register

**Project:** Citizen Cybercrime Intelligence & Reporting Platform (CCIP) / Astraea Enclave  
**Document Purpose:** Risk Identification, Threat Assessment, and Mitigation Tracking  
**Status:** Active Governance Baseline (Phase L)  

---

## 1. Risk Evaluation Matrix

| Risk ID & Title | Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1: Cyber NER OOD Weakness** | The token classifier experiences severe performance drops (down to 3.23% F1) on novel, informal, or adversarial phrasings. | **High** | **Medium** | Enforce deterministic structured indicator authority (regex strictly overrides transformer tokens); prioritize real-world dataset acquisition in Phase M. | ML Lead | **MITIGATED IN ARCHITECTURE** |
| **R2: Synthetic / Bootstrap Reliance** | Current models trained on synthetic narratives may not fully capture nuanced regional dialect or emerging crime types. | **High** | **Medium** | Frame all current metrics transparently as bootstrap baselines; execute structured 4-phase real-data validation plan. | ML Lead | **ACTIVE MONITORING** |
| **R3: Correlation False Positives** | High-volume fraud types (e.g. general investment scams) may be falsely correlated based on similar phrasing without common suspects. | **Medium** | **High** | Require tri-layered correlation: semantic similarity + exact structured entity overlap + sequence cross-encoder; enforce mandatory investigator review. | Lead Architect | **MITIGATED IN PIPELINE** |
| **R4: Evidence Graph False Links** | Candidate link prediction may propose incorrect multi-hop edges, cluttering the investigator's visual network. | **Medium** | **Low** | Initialize all candidate links with `review_status="CANDIDATE_LINK"`; visually differentiate via dashed lines; mandate explicit officer confirmation. | Full-Stack Lead | **MITIGATED IN UI** |
| **R5: Model Probability Miscalibration** | Softmax confidence scores may be overconfident or poorly calibrated, leading to false urgency ratings. | **Medium** | **Medium** | Implement temperature scaling and Platt scaling; evaluate models against Expected Calibration Error (ECE < 0.05). | ML Engineer | **PLANNED FOR PHASE M** |
| **R6: Dataset Privacy & Re-Identification** | Complaints stored or processed during training might inadvertently expose victim PII or sensitive banking credentials. | **Low** | **Critical** | Automated regex/NER redaction; two-tier de-identification pipeline; pseudonymization of identifiers; offline isolated storage. | Security Officer | **CONTROLLED** |
| **R7: Legal & Policy Constraints** | Police departments or government entities may have specific statutory procurement or evidentiary compliance rules. | **Medium** | **High** | Restrict all AI outputs to advisory investigative leads; do not use AI outputs as primary court exhibits; conduct formal departmental legal review. | Legal / Policy | **POLICY ENFORCED** |
| **R8: Infrastructure Scalability** | Concurrent multi-user load during peak incident hours could cause CPU exhaustion or latency spikes on single-host deployments. | **Medium** | **Medium** | Migrate from SQLite to PostgreSQL with read replicas; decouple inference workers behind Redis queues; deploy GPU acceleration. | DevOps Lead | **PLANNED FOR PILOT** |
| **R9: Model Artifact Storage Integrity** | Model weight files on local disk could be corrupted or tampered with by unauthorized host processes. | **Low** | **High** | Verify cryptographic SHA-256 digests of `model.safetensors` against certified manifests during model load; restrict filesystem permissions. | Security Officer | **PARTIALLY MITIGATED** |
| **R10: Investigator Trust & Adoption** | Police officers may distrust AI suggestions or reject the software due to workflow friction. | **Medium** | **High** | Involve active investigators in Phase P1 shadow-mode testing; provide clear explainable rationales; lock human review verdicts permanently. | Product Lead | **ACTIVE ENGAGEMENT** |
| **R11: Adversarial Lure Inputs** | Sophisticated fraudsters may submit adversarial prompt injections or malformed text to poison the triage engine. | **Low** | **Medium** | Input length truncation (5000 chars); strict Pydantic payload validation; deterministic regex extraction immune to prompt injection. | Security Officer | **MITIGATED IN CODE** |
| **R12: Long-Term Model Distribution Drift** | Fraud syndicates continuously pivot to new platforms and payment methods, causing model decay over time. | **High** | **Medium** | Quarantined learning candidate queue capturing vetted operational outcomes; 5-hard-gate filter; regular scheduled offline re-benchmarking. | ML Lead | **GOVERNED** |
| **R13: Pilot Operational Support Overhead** | Running an active police pilot requires ongoing technical support, user onboarding, and incident troubleshooting. | **Medium** | **Medium** | Provide comprehensive Demo Runbook, step-by-step training manuals, and designated pilot engineering on-call rotation. | Pilot Liaison | **DOCUMENTED** |
| **R14: Security Exposure Prior to Pilot** | Exposing the prototype web interface to live networks without reverse proxy WAF, TLS, and malware scanning. | **Low** | **Critical** | Complete Pilot Security Checklist: deploy Nginx reverse proxy with TLS 1.3, rate limiting, and ClamAV antivirus daemon on evidence uploads. | Security Officer | **CHECKLIST LOCKED** |
