# Commercial Business Model & Go-to-Market Options

**Project:** Citizen Cybercrime Intelligence & Reporting Platform (CCIP) / Astraea Enclave  
**Document Purpose:** Commercial Strategy, Revenue Models, and Procurement Evaluation  
**Note:** All monetary figures and pricing bands presented herein are *strictly illustrative* for financial modeling and do not represent existing commercial quotes or contracts.

## 2. Business Model Options Evaluation

| Model Option | Target Customer Profile | Revenue Structure | Commercial Advantages | Operational & Procurement Risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Option A: Government Enterprise License** | State Police Cyber Cells, National Crime Records Bureau | Annual site license ($75k–$250k / year illustrative) covering an entire state police department. | Matches public-sector procurement cycles (annual budgetary allocations); covers unlimited citizen complaint volume. | Long procurement cycles (6–18 months); RFP tender dependencies; complex bureaucratic approvals. |
| **Option B: Per-Investigator Seat Licensing** | Municipal police cyber units, regional cyber stations, private detective agencies | Monthly or annual fee per investigator seat ($100–$250 / seat / month illustrative). | Low friction to pilot entry; allows department to start with a small pilot team and expand organically. | Public sector agencies resist variable per-seat software costs; complex license key enforcement. |
| **Option C: Turnkey Enclave Appliance** | Banks, financial institutions, enterprise digital risk units | One-time hardware/software setup fee ($50k illustrative) + 20% annual maintenance fee. | Appeals to security teams requiring air-gapped on-premise hardware; predictable capital expenditure (CapEx). | Hardware logistics, warranty management, and physical maintenance overhead. |
| **Option D: Pilot-to-Contract (Paid Pilot)** | Forward-looking law-enforcement innovation grants, university research SOCs | 6-month fixed-price evaluation pilot ($25k–$50k illustrative) creditable against future license. | Generates upfront revenue; secures high organizational commitment; establishes operational proof points. | Requires intense engineering support during pilot; conversion depends on departmental leadership changes. |

## 3. Professional Services & Ancillary Revenue Streams

1. **Custom System Integration & Data Ingestion ($20k–$50k illustrative per deployment):**
   - Developing custom ingestion adaptors connecting CCIP to legacy police RMS databases (e.g. CCTNS in India, NIBRS in the US).
2. **Domain-Specific Model Fine-Tuning ($30k–$60k illustrative per domain):**
   - Fine-tuning the Hybrid NER and Relation models on proprietary banking, regional language, or specialized industry datasets using isolated offline training pipelines.
3. **Annual Software Maintenance & Support (SMA) (18%–22% of license fee):**
   - Guaranteed SLA support, regular vulnerability updates, and quarterly model checkpoint refreshes.

---

## 4. Recommended Commercial Trajectory

1. **Stage 1 (Current Stage): Academic & Innovation Grant Pilots (Months 1–6)**
   - Deploy as a zero-software-cost pilot with 1–2 university cyber labs or state cyber cell innovation partnerships to establish empirical real-world validation.
2. **Stage 2: Paid Pilot-to-Contract in 2 Designated Police Jurisdictions (Months 7–12)**
   - Transition successful sandboxes into funded pilot evaluations supported by government cybercrime grants.
3. **Stage 3: Full State-Wide Enterprise Licensing (Months 13–24)**
   - Participate in formal public tenders for state-level cybercrime intelligence orchestration.
